FAQ

Questions? Answers.

What exactly is NODR?

NODR is a decentralized utility network for the internet. Instead of relying only on giant data centers, NODR pools storage, bandwidth, compute and power from everyday people and businesses — and pays them for it.

What hardware do I need?

Anything from a spare laptop or Raspberry Pi to a home NAS or a rack of servers. Each node type has minimum requirements; the client checks your setup and tells you what you can run.

How do I get paid?

Your node is verified with cryptographic proofs of storage, uptime and delivery. Rewards are calculated every epoch based on the real work you did, and you can withdraw them whenever you like.

Is my data and my network safe?

Everything that touches your node is encrypted and sharded, so you never see anyone's files — and they never see yours. You control exactly how much disk, bandwidth and compute you share.

Who uses the network?

Developers, apps and AI teams who need cheaper, more resilient infrastructure that lives closer to their users. Their payments fund the rewards node operators earn.
